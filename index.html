About Me


Hi, I'm Bertan, a master's student in Computer Science at the University of Bonn. I have focused my studies on machine learning and computer vision, and I am particularly interested in learning-based approaches to 3D vision problems, including 3D reconstruction, scene understanding, and 3D multi-object tracking. Currently, I am preparing my master's thesis.

I previously completed my bachelor's degree in Computer Science at the University of Bonn. During my master's studies, I worked as a student assistant at the Autonomous Intelligent Systems Group, supervised by Prof. Sven Behnke. There, I developed and integrated perception components for autonomous service robots, including dense depth estimation for fisheye cameras, person detection and re-identification, and multi-sensor 3D tracking. I contributed to the NimbRo team's participation in the RoboCup@Home competitions.

Outside of my work and studies, I am passionate about music, video games, and literature.
